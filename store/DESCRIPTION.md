# PBL-7 — appstore listing

**Tagline:** A landscape shmup through the solar system.

**Short description (≤ 140 chars):**
Hold your Pebble Time 2 sideways and fly PBL-7 to Earth. Rise, fire, dodge, and beat a boss on every planet. 11 planets, secrets, 5 languages.

**Full description:**

PBL-7 is a sideways-held arcade shoot-'em-up for the Pebble Time 2. Turn your
watch a quarter turn — the three buttons line up along the bottom and the
screen becomes a 228×200 landscape playfield.

You are PBL-7, a Pebblonian explorer carrying the Time Core to Earth to save
it. Every planet mistakes you for an invader and sends its champion to stop
you. Hold UP to rise, release to fall, DOWN to fire (don't hold it too long or
the gun overheats and jams), and SELECT for a screen-clearing smart bomb.

Fight your way past a themed boss on each of Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter,
Saturn, Uranus and Neptune — each with its own gimmick, from acid clouds and
gravity wells to splitting rings and lightning storms. After every boss you
choose one of three Pebble-themed upgrades that carry through the run. Hidden
on each planet is a lost Pebble device to collect — find all eight to unlock
the true ending, and beat Neptune without taking a hit to open a secret portal.

FEATURES
• Landscape gameplay — held sideways, a real quarter-turn shmup.
• 11 planets: Mercury → Neptune, a secret boss-rush, a hidden ally world, a
  Moon base and a three-phase Earth finale.
• A themed boss with a unique gimmick on every planet.
• Upgrade cards after each boss, 8 hidden collectible Pebbles, and two
  secret endings.
• Weapon-heat system: burst-fire beats mashing — or switch the jam off in
  Settings for easy mode.
• Settings: backlight, weapon jam (easy mode), rumble, sound, reset save.
• Made for the Pebble Time 2 (Emery) colour screen; plays entirely offline.
• Also plays on the round Pebble Round 2 (Gabbro). A round screen looks the
  same in any orientation, so there you hold the watch normally; no sound.
• Available in English, Nederlands, Français, Deutsch and Español, following
  your watch's system language automatically.
• Progress, records and collectibles saved on the watch.
